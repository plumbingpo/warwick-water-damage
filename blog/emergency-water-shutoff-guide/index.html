Finding Your Water Shutoff Valve in Warwick, RI

Home / Blog / How to Shut Off Your Water in an Emergency in Warwick, RI

When water is pouring into your home, shutting it off limits the damage faster than anything else. Finding and working the water shutoff valve is a skill that could keep thousands of dollars in your pocket and spare your property a lot of damage. If you own a home in Warwick, RI, find your shutoff valve and practice using it now, not during a leak.

Where to Find the Main Water Shutoff

The main shutoff in a typical Warwick home sits in one of five places: a laundry area or utility room, a crawl space or basement by the foundation wall, an interior wall of the garage, the front of the home at the point the line comes in from the street, or a ground-level box by the street that holds the meter shutoff. Expect to find either a ball valve with a lever handle or a gate valve with a round handle. A ball valve is the more reliable of the two and closes with a quarter turn.

The Quick Way to Stop the Water

A gate valve closes when you turn its handle clockwise, to the right, for several full turns, to the point where it will not go further. A ball valve is off when its lever sits perpendicular to the pipe, so turn it to that position. A valve that will not move should not be forced, as breaking it would make things worse. Go to the meter and close it there with a meter key or an adjustable wrench.

Individual Fixture Shutoffs

A separate valve on most appliances and fixtures lets you cut water to just that spot while the rest of the house stays on. Check behind toilets and washing machines, beneath each sink, and next to the water heater. Once you know where they are, a local leak can be stopped fast while every other part of the home keeps its water service.
