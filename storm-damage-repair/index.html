Signs It Is Time for Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Ceiling drywall that sags or bulges, or paint that bubbles after rain
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • Gutters or downspouts torn loose, bent or dumping water against the house
  • Wet insulation in the attic or wall cavities found after bad weather
  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up
  • Missing, damaged or lifted shingles you can see from the ground
  • Water pooling in the attic, around the chimney or near skylights

Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Learn where the main water shutoff is and how to close it if pipes are damaged in a storm
  • Install impact-resistant windows or storm shutters if you live in a hurricane-prone area
  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often
  • Branches break in high wind, so keep nearby trees pruned away from the roof and siding
  • Photograph your property once a year so its condition is on record for insurance purposes
  • Ice dams form when attics run warm, so check that yours has enough insulation and airflow
